## explain

Q: Gradient clipping nima?
A: Gradient clipping — gradient juda katta bo‘lib ketsa, uni belgilangan chegaragacha kichraytirish. Bu o‘qitish paytida loss birdan sakrab ketishining oldini oladi.

Q: Warmup nima?
A: Warmup — o‘qitish boshida learning rate'ni noldan asta-sekin ko‘tarish. Model hali tasodifiy holatda bo‘lganda katta qadamlar uni buzib qo‘ymasligi uchun ishlatiladi.

Q: Weight decay nima?
A: Weight decay — har qadamda og‘irliklarni biroz kichraytirib boradigan regularizatsiya usuli. U og‘irliklar haddan tashqari katta bo‘lishiga yo‘l qo‘ymaydi va overfittingni kamaytiradi.

Q: Bias-variance tradeoff nima?
A: Bias-variance tradeoff — model juda sodda bo‘lsa xatosi katta (bias), juda murakkab bo‘lsa yangi ma’lumotda beqaror (variance) bo‘lishi. Yaxshi model shu ikkisi o‘rtasidagi muvozanatni topadi.

Q: KV cache nima?
A: KV cache — generatsiya paytida oldingi tokenlar uchun hisoblangan key va value vektorlarini saqlab qo‘yish. Shunda har yangi tokenda ularni qaytadan hisoblash shart bo‘lmaydi va javob tezroq chiqadi.

Q: Beam search nima?
A: Beam search — har qadamda bitta emas, bir nechta eng ehtimolli davomni saqlab boradigan decoding usuli. Oxirida umumiy ehtimolligi eng yuqori bo‘lgan matn tanlanadi.

Q: Top-p sampling nima?
A: Top-p sampling — ehtimolliklari yig‘indisi p ga yetguncha eng ehtimolli tokenlarni olib, faqat ular orasidan tasodifiy tanlash. Masalan, p = 0.9 bo‘lsa, juda kam ehtimolli tokenlar umuman tanlanmaydi.

Q: Top-k sampling nima?
A: Top-k sampling — har qadamda faqat eng ehtimolli k ta token orasidan tasodifiy tanlash. Masalan, k = 50 bo‘lsa, qolgan tokenlar e’tiborga olinmaydi.

Q: Perplexity nima?
A: Perplexity — til modeli matnni qanchalik yaxshi bashorat qilishini ko‘rsatadigan metrika. Qiymati qancha kichik bo‘lsa, model matndan shuncha kam "hayron qoladi", ya’ni yaxshiroq.

Q: BLEU metrikasi nima?
A: BLEU — mashina tarjimasini baholash metrikasi. U model tarjimasidagi so‘z birikmalari odam qilgan tarjimaga qanchalik mos kelishini 0 dan 1 gacha son bilan o‘lchaydi.

Q: Instruction tuning nima?
A: Instruction tuning — modelni "ko‘rsatma va javob" juftliklarida o‘qitish. Shundan keyin model matnni shunchaki davom ettirmay, berilgan topshiriqni bajarishni o‘rganadi.

Q: Catastrophic forgetting nima?
A: Catastrophic forgetting — model yangi vazifaga o‘qitilganda oldin bilgan narsalarini unutib qo‘yishi. LoRA kabi usullar base og‘irliklarni o‘zgartirmagani uchun bu muammoni kamaytiradi.

Q: Pruning nima?
A: Pruning — modeldagi ahamiyati kam og‘irlik yoki neyronlarni olib tashlash. Model kichikroq va tezroq bo‘ladi, sifat esa ozgina pasayadi.

Q: Mixed precision training nima?
A: Mixed precision training — hisoblashlarning ko‘p qismini 16 bitda, muhim qismlarini esa 32 bitda bajarish. Bu xotirani tejaydi va GPU'da o‘qitishni tezlashtiradi.

Q: Gradient accumulation nima?
A: Gradient accumulation — bir necha kichik qadamning gradientlarini yig‘ib, keyin bitta yangilash qilish. Xotira kichik bo‘lsa ham, katta to‘plam bilan o‘qitgandek natija beradi.

Q: Gradient checkpointing nima?
A: Gradient checkpointing — oraliq natijalarni saqlamay, backpropagation paytida qaytadan hisoblash. O‘qitish biroz sekinlashadi, lekin GPU xotirasi ancha tejaladi.

Q: Zero-shot prompting nima?
A: Zero-shot prompting — modelga birorta ham namuna bermay, faqat topshiriqning o‘zini yozish. Masalan: "Shu gapning kayfiyatini aniqla: ijobiy yoki salbiy."

Q: Chain-of-thought nima?
A: Chain-of-thought — modeldan javobdan oldin fikrlash qadamlarini yozishni so‘rash. Bu ayniqsa ko‘p bosqichli masalalarda javob sifatini oshiradi.

Q: Stop token nima?
A: Stop token — model javobni tugatganini bildiradigan maxsus token. Model shu tokenni chiqarganda generatsiya to‘xtaydi.

Q: Attention mask nima?
A: Attention mask — modelga qaysi tokenlarga e’tibor berish kerakligini ko‘rsatadigan 0 va 1 lardan iborat ro‘yxat. Masalan, padding tokenlari 0 bilan belgilanadi va hisobga olinmaydi.

Q: Learning curve nima?
A: Learning curve — o‘qitish davomida train va validation loss qanday o‘zgarishini ko‘rsatadigan grafik. Validation loss ko‘tarila boshlasa, overfitting boshlangan bo‘ladi.

Q: Class weights nima?
A: Class weights — kam uchraydigan sinfdagi xatolarga lossda kattaroq og‘irlik berish. Bu nomutanosib datasetda model faqat ko‘p sinfni tanlab qo‘ymasligiga yordam beradi.

Q: Stratified split nima?
A: Stratified split — datasetni bo‘lishda har bir qismda sinflar ulushini bir xil saqlash. Masalan, datasetda 10% spam bo‘lsa, train va testda ham taxminan 10% spam bo‘ladi.

Q: Optimizer nima?
A: Optimizer — gradientlar asosida model og‘irliklarini qanday yangilashni hal qiladigan algoritm. Eng mashhurlari SGD va Adam.

Q: Clustering nima?
A: Clustering — yorliqsiz ma’lumotlarni o‘xshashligiga qarab guruhlarga ajratish. Masalan, xaridorlarni xarid odatlariga qarab guruhlash.

Q: Model parametrlari nima?
A: Model parametrlari — o‘qitish davomida o‘rganiladigan sonlar, ya’ni og‘irliklar va bias'lar. Masalan, Qwen2.5-0.5B modelida taxminan 500 million parametr bor.

Q: Sun’iy neyron nima?
A: Sun’iy neyron — kirish qiymatlarini og‘irliklarga ko‘paytirib qo‘shadigan va natijani aktivatsiya funksiyasidan o‘tkazadigan kichik hisoblash birligi. Neyron tarmoq shunday neyronlardan tuzilgan.

Q: VRAM nima?
A: VRAM — GPU'ning o‘z xotirasi. Model va o‘qitish paytidagi oraliq natijalar shu xotiraga sig‘ishi kerak, shuning uchun katta modellar ko‘p VRAM talab qiladi.

Q: Learning rate scheduler nima?
A: Learning rate scheduler — o‘qitish davomida learning rate'ni o‘zgartirib boradigan qoida. Masalan, avval warmup bilan ko‘tarib, keyin nolgacha asta pasaytirish.

Q: Scaling laws nima?
A: Scaling laws — model hajmi, data miqdori va hisoblash kuchi oshganda loss qanday kamayishini ko‘rsatadigan qonuniyatlar. Ular qancha katta model uchun qancha data kerakligini rejalashtirishga yordam beradi.

Q: Mixture of Experts nima?
A: Mixture of Experts (MoE) — modelda bir nechta "ekspert" qatlam bo‘lib, har bir token uchun ulardan faqat bir nechtasi ishlaydigan arxitektura. Shunda model katta bo‘lsa ham, har bir token uchun hisoblash kam bo‘ladi.

Q: Byte Pair Encoding nima?
A: Byte Pair Encoding (BPE) — matnni bo‘laklarga ajratish usuli: eng ko‘p uchraydigan belgi juftliklari bosqichma-bosqich bitta tokenga birlashtiriladi. Natijada ko‘p uchraydigan so‘zlar bitta token, kam uchraydiganlari esa bir necha bo‘lak bo‘ladi.

Q: Batch normalization nima?
A: Batch normalization — qatlam chiqishlarini har bir to‘plam bo‘yicha o‘rtachasi 0, dispersiyasi 1 bo‘ladigan qilib normallashtirish. Bu chuqur tarmoqlarni barqarorroq va tezroq o‘qitadi.

Q: Convolution nima?
A: Convolution — kichik filtrni rasm ustida siljitib, har bir joyda naqsh (masalan, chegara yoki burchak) bor-yo‘qligini hisoblash. CNN modellar shu amal asosida ishlaydi.

Q: Pooling qatlami nima?
A: Pooling qatlami — rasm o‘lchamini kichraytiradigan qatlam: kichik sohadagi qiymatlardan bittasini (masalan, eng kattasini) qoldiradi. Bu hisoblashni kamaytiradi va muhim belgilarni saqlaydi.

Q: Encoder nima?
A: Encoder — kirish matnini ma’noni ifodalovchi vektorlarga aylantiradigan model qismi. U tasniflash va qidiruv kabi vazifalarda ishlatiladi.

Q: Decoder nima?
A: Decoder — oldingi tokenlarga qarab keyingi tokenni bashorat qiladigan model qismi. GPT, Llama va Qwen kabi LLM'lar faqat decoder'dan iborat.

Q: Seq2seq model nima?
A: Seq2seq model — bir ketma-ketlikni boshqasiga aylantiradigan model, masalan, gapni bir tildan boshqa tilga tarjima qilish. U odatda encoder va decoder'dan iborat bo‘ladi.

Q: Diffusion model nima?
A: Diffusion model — shovqindan boshlab, uni qadam-baqadam tozalab rasm hosil qiladigan generativ model. Matndan rasm yaratuvchi ko‘p modellar shu usulda ishlaydi.

Q: Autoencoder nima?
A: Autoencoder — kirish ma’lumotini kichik vektorga siqib, keyin undan qayta tiklashni o‘rganadigan neyron tarmoq. U o‘lchamni kamaytirish va anomaliyalarni topishda ishlatiladi.

Q: Softmax nima?
A: Softmax — modelning xom sonlarini (logit) yig‘indisi 1 ga teng ehtimolliklarga aylantiradigan funksiya. LLM shu ehtimolliklar asosida keyingi tokenni tanlaydi.

Q: ReLU funksiyasi nima?
A: ReLU — manfiy qiymatlarni 0 ga aylantiradigan, musbatlarini esa o‘zgarishsiz qoldiradigan aktivatsiya funksiyasi. U sodda va tez bo‘lgani uchun chuqur tarmoqlarda keng ishlatiladi.

Q: Residual connection nima?
A: Residual connection — qatlam kirishini uning chiqishiga to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri qo‘shish. Bu juda chuqur tarmoqlarda gradient yo‘qolib ketmasligiga yordam beradi.

Q: Positional encoding nima?
A: Positional encoding — har bir tokenga uning gapdagi o‘rni haqida ma’lumot qo‘shish. Usiz Transformer "it mushukni quvdi" va "mushuk itni quvdi" gaplarini farqlay olmasdi.

Q: Knowledge distillation nima?
A: Knowledge distillation — katta "o‘qituvchi" model javoblari asosida kichik "talaba" modelni o‘qitish. Kichik model tezroq ishlaydi va katta modelning bilimining bir qismini saqlab qoladi.

Q: Early stopping nima?
A: Early stopping — validation loss yaxshilanishni to‘xtatganda o‘qitishni to‘xtatish. Bu overfittingning oldini oladi va vaqtni tejaydi.

Q: Logit nima?
A: Logit — model chiqargan, hali ehtimollikka aylantirilmagan xom son. Softmax logitlarni ehtimolliklarga aylantiradi.

Q: Cross-entropy loss nima?
A: Cross-entropy loss — model to‘g‘ri javobga qancha past ehtimollik bergan bo‘lsa, shuncha katta bo‘ladigan xato o‘lchovi. LLM'lar keyingi tokenni bashorat qilishni shu loss bilan o‘rganadi.

Q: What is gradient clipping?
A: Gradient clipping limits the size of the gradient during training. If a gradient becomes too large, it is scaled down, so the loss does not suddenly jump.

Q: What is weight decay?
A: Weight decay is a regularization method that shrinks the weights a little at every step. It keeps the weights small and reduces overfitting.

Q: What is a KV cache?
A: A KV cache stores the key and value vectors of earlier tokens during generation. The model does not have to compute them again for every new token, so answers come out faster.

Q: What is perplexity?
A: Perplexity measures how well a language model predicts a text. The lower it is, the less "surprised" the model is, which means it is better.

Q: What is instruction tuning?
A: Instruction tuning trains a model on pairs of instructions and answers. After it, the model follows the task instead of just continuing the text.

Q: What is catastrophic forgetting?
A: Catastrophic forgetting is when a model loses what it knew after being trained on a new task. Methods like LoRA reduce it because the base weights stay frozen.

Q: What is gradient accumulation?
A: Gradient accumulation adds up the gradients of several small steps and then makes one update. It gives the effect of a larger training batch when memory is limited.

Q: What is zero-shot prompting?
A: Zero-shot prompting means giving the model a task with no examples. For example: "Is this review positive or negative?"

Q: What is chain-of-thought prompting?
A: Chain-of-thought prompting asks the model to write its reasoning steps before the answer. It often improves answers to multi-step problems.

Q: What is an optimizer?
A: An optimizer is the algorithm that updates the model's weights using the gradients. The most common ones are SGD and Adam.

Q: What is clustering?
A: Clustering groups unlabeled data by similarity. For example, it can group customers by their shopping habits.

Q: What is softmax?
A: Softmax turns the model's raw scores (logits) into probabilities that add up to 1. An LLM uses these probabilities to choose the next token.

Q: What is a residual connection?
A: A residual connection adds a layer's input directly to its output. It helps gradients flow through very deep networks.

Q: What is knowledge distillation?
A: Knowledge distillation trains a small "student" model on the outputs of a large "teacher" model. The student is faster and keeps part of the teacher's knowledge.

Q: What is early stopping?
A: Early stopping ends training when the validation loss stops improving. It prevents overfitting and saves time.

Q: What is a decoder-only model?
A: A decoder-only model predicts the next token from the previous ones. GPT, Llama and Qwen are decoder-only models.

Q: What is a stop token?
A: A stop token is a special token that marks the end of an answer. Generation stops when the model produces it.

Q: What is mixed precision training?
A: Mixed precision training does most calculations in 16 bits and the sensitive parts in 32 bits. It saves memory and makes training faster on a GPU.

Q: What is a learning curve?
A: A learning curve is a plot of training and validation loss over time. When the validation loss starts to rise, overfitting has begun.

Q: What is a diffusion model?
A: A diffusion model creates an image by starting from noise and removing it step by step. Many text-to-image models work this way.
